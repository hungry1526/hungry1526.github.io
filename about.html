Biography

I am HJ Hang, a product manager at NVIDIA, where I work on projects that contribute toward Artificial General Intelligence. My work spans large language models, autonomous agents, and world models, with a particular focus on turning research advances into deployable systems and infrastructure.

Before NVIDIA, I built products across the cloud-native and AI infrastructure stack, including Azure Kubernetes Service, KAITO, Azure Arc, Azure Edge Zone, and Ant Group's Graph Database, among others.

Earlier in my career, I spent several years in venture capital investing in infrastructure, database, and security companies.
